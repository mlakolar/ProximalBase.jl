Total variation, isotonic regression and SLOPE

Penalties that depend on the order of the entries.

Total variation, monotonicity and the sorted ℓ1 norm pool adjacent entries into blocks of equal values. Three problems:

  • a piecewise-constant signal by total-variation denoising and the fused lasso, whose solutions are proximal operators;
  • isotonic regression of a monotone trend, the projection onto the nondecreasing vectors;
  • SLOPE regression, which gives correlated predictors equal coefficients where the lasso picks among them.
using ProximalBase, LinearAlgebra, Random

rng = Xoshiro(18)
Random.Xoshiro(0xcc369a38fa298e7d, 0xdd92787af0c09ba2, 0x123de3b970906fdd, 0x1018f86935cee8dc, 0x30d2412977e5eeb8)

A piecewise-constant signal

Total-variation denoising minimizes ‖y - z‖²/2 + λ Σᵢ |zᵢ₊₁ - zᵢ|: its solution is the proximal operator of TotalVariation1D(λ) at y with step 1.

n = 200
signal = [zeros(50); fill(2.0, 40); fill(-1.0, 60); fill(1.0, 50)]
y = signal .+ 0.5 .* randn(rng, n)
λ = 4.0
z = prox(TotalVariation1D(λ), y)
jumps = abs.(diff(z))
largest = sort(partialsortperm(jumps, 1:3; rev = true))
println("total variation: ", count(>(0), jumps), " jumps, the largest three after ", largest, "; error ",
        round(norm(z - signal) / sqrt(n), digits = 3), " against ", round(norm(y - signal) / sqrt(n), digits = 3),
        " for the data")
@assert largest == [50, 90, 150]                 # the change points
@assert norm(z - signal) < norm(y - signal) / 2
total variation: 11 jumps, the largest three after [50, 90, 150]; error 0.205 against 0.495 for the data

the optimality conditions: the cumulative sums of the residual stay within ±λ and end at zero

r = cumsum(y - z)
@assert all(abs.(r) .<= λ + 1e-9) && abs(r[end]) < 1e-9

the fused lasso adds λ1‖z‖₁, which soft-thresholds the denoised signal: the first segment, whose level is 0, becomes exactly zero up to the step at 49

zf = prox(FusedLasso(0.3, λ), y)
@assert zf ≈ shrink.(z, 0.3)
println("fused lasso: ", count(iszero, zf[1:50]), " of the first 50 entries are zero")
@assert all(iszero, zf[1:49])
fused lasso: 49 of the first 50 entries are zero

Isotonic regression

The nondecreasing fit nearest to the data, the projection onto the monotone vectors, pools the entries that violate the order into blocks at their means.

t = sort(rand(rng, 100))
y = log1p.(5 .* t) .+ 0.2 .* randn(rng, 100)
z = prox(IndIsotonic(), y)
println("isotonic regression: ", length(unique(z)), " blocks for 100 points")
@assert issorted(z)
isotonic regression: 21 blocks for 100 points

the optimality conditions: the cumulative sums of the residual are nonnegative, and zero where a block ends

r = cumsum(y - z)
ends = [findall(!iszero, diff(z)); 100]
@assert all(r .>= -1e-10) && all(abs.(r[ends]) .< 1e-10)

SLOPE regression

Three columns of X are nearly equal. The lasso splits their effect unevenly; SLOPE, whose sorted ℓ1 norm pools coefficients of equal magnitude, gives them one coefficient.

n, p = 100, 30
Z = randn(rng, n, p)
X = copy(Z)
X[:, 2:3] .= Z[:, 1] .+ 0.05 .* Z[:, 2:3]
β_true = [1.0; 1.0; 1.0; zeros(p - 6); -1.5; 0.0; 0.0]
y = X * β_true + 0.5 * randn(rng, n)
f = (1 / n) * LeastSquaresLoss(y, X)

function fista(f, g, p; iters = 20000)
    L = lipschitz(f)
    β, βold, z, grad = zeros(p), zeros(p), zeros(p), zeros(p)
    t = 1.0
    for _ in 1:iters
        gradient!(grad, f, z)
        copyto!(βold, β)
        β .= z .- grad ./ L
        prox!(β, g, β, 1 / L)
        tnew = (1 + sqrt(1 + 4t^2)) / 2
        z .= β .+ ((t - 1) / tnew) .* (β .- βold)
        t = tnew
    end
    β
end

βl = fista(f, NormL1(0.1), p)
g = SLOPE(0.1 .* collect(range(2.0, 0.2; length = p)))   # a decreasing sequence
βs = fista(f, g, p)
println("lasso: β[1:3] = ", round.(βl[1:3], digits = 3), "; SLOPE: β[1:3] = ", round.(βs[1:3], digits = 3))
@assert βs[1] ≈ βs[2] ≈ βs[3] && maximum(βl[1:3]) > 2minimum(βl[1:3])
@assert findall(!iszero, βs) == [1, 2, 3, p - 2]
@assert norm(gradient_mapping(f, g, βs, 1 / lipschitz(f))) < 1e-9
lasso: β[1:3] = [0.0, 1.107, 1.805]; SLOPE: β[1:3] = [0.948, 0.948, 0.948]
