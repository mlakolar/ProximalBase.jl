The ℓ1 norm and its relatives

For NormL1, $g(x) = \lambda_0\sum_i w_i|x_i|$, the problem defining the proximal operator separates over the coordinates. Each scalar problem $\min_z \gamma\lambda_0 w_i|z| + \tfrac12(z - v_i)^2$ is solved by soft thresholding at $\gamma\lambda_0 w_i$. A weight $w_i = 0$ leaves the coordinate unpenalized, as for an intercept. The subdifferential of $|\cdot|$ at zero is $[-1, 1]$, so given the gradient $g_i$ of the smooth part, the coordinate $x_i = 0$ is optimal exactly when $|g_i| \le \lambda_0 w_i$. kkt_violation returns $|g_i| - \lambda_0 w_i$: positive when the coordinate should leave zero.

NormL1Nonneg adds the constraint $x \ge 0$, which turns soft thresholding one-sided. ElasticNet adds $\tfrac{\lambda_2}{2}\|x\|^2$. Completing the square, its proximal operator is soft thresholding followed by the shrinkage $1/(1 + \gamma\lambda_2)$.

Nonconvex penalties

ℓ0. The scalar problem $\min_z \gamma\lambda\,\mathbf{1}[z \ne 0] + \tfrac12(z - v)^2$ compares $z = 0$, of value $\tfrac12 v^2$, with $z = v$, of value $\gamma\lambda$. So the proximal operator keeps $v$ when $|v| > \sqrt{2\gamma\lambda}$ and sets it to zero otherwise.

MCP (Zhang, 2010). The penalty is $p(t) = \lambda|t| - t^2/(2a)$ for $|t| \le a\lambda$ and $a\lambda^2/2$ beyond, with $a > 1$. For $\gamma < a$ the scalar problem is convex, and its solution is firm thresholding:

\[\operatorname{prox}_{\gamma p}(v) = \begin{cases} 0 & |v| \le \gamma\lambda,\\ \operatorname{sign}(v)\,\dfrac{|v| - \gamma\lambda}{1 - \gamma/a} & \gamma\lambda < |v| \le a\lambda,\\ v & |v| > a\lambda . \end{cases}\]

Large values pass untouched, so MCP does not shrink large coefficients the way the ℓ1 norm does.

SCAD (Fan and Li, 2001). The penalty is $\lambda|t|$ for $|t| \le \lambda$, $(2a\lambda|t| - t^2 - \lambda^2)/(2(a - 1))$ for $\lambda < |t| \le a\lambda$, and $(a + 1)\lambda^2/2$ beyond, with $a > 2$. For $\gamma < a - 1$,

\[\operatorname{prox}_{\gamma p}(v) = \begin{cases} S_{\gamma\lambda}(v) & |v| \le (1 + \gamma)\lambda,\\ \dfrac{(a - 1)v - \operatorname{sign}(v)\,a\gamma\lambda}{a - 1 - \gamma} & (1 + \gamma)\lambda < |v| \le a\lambda,\\ v & |v| > a\lambda , \end{cases}\]

which for $\gamma = 1$ is Fan and Li's thresholding rule. Each piece comes from setting the derivative of $\gamma p(z) + \tfrac12(z - v)^2$ to zero on the corresponding interval.

For larger steps the scalar problems of MCP and SCAD are not convex. prox_entry still returns an exact minimizer: it compares the candidate minimizers of the pieces. So these penalties work in coordinate descent and proximal gradient methods with any step. At zero their subdifferential is that of $\lambda|\cdot|$, so kkt_violation is $|g_i| - \lambda$.

Norms of vectors and groups

ℓ2. For $g = \lambda\|\cdot\|_2$, the proximal point lies on the segment from $0$ to $v$. Minimizing over its length gives block soft thresholding, $\max(1 - \gamma\lambda/\|v\|_2, 0)\,v$, which sets the whole vector to zero when $\|v\|_2 \le \gamma\lambda$.

ℓ∞. The conjugate of $\lambda\|\cdot\|_\infty$ is the indicator of the ℓ1 ball of radius $\lambda$. So by Moreau's decomposition its proximal operator is $v - P_{\|\cdot\|_1 \le \gamma\lambda}(v)$, through the ℓ1-ball projection of Sets.

Groups. GroupNormL2(λ0, groups, w) is $\lambda_0\sum_k w_k\|x_{G_k}\|_2$ over disjoint groups, the group lasso penalty. By default $w_k = \sqrt{|G_k|}$, and indices in no group are unpenalized. Its proximal operator applies block soft thresholding with threshold $\gamma\lambda_0 w_k$ to each group. A group at zero is optimal when $\|g_{G_k}\|_2 \le \lambda_0 w_k$. GroupNormLinf uses the ℓ∞ norm of each group, with the ℓ1-ball projection, and its optimality test is $\|g_{G_k}\|_1 \le \lambda_0 w_k$. On an AtomIterate that shares their groups, these penalties update one group at a time.

The rows of a $p \times k$ matrix are the groups [i:p:p*k for i in 1:p] of its linear indices. So GroupNormL2 with these groups is the row-sparse penalty of multitask regression; see the multitask example.

Fused pairs. NormL1Fused(λ1, λ2) acts on pairs of arrays $(a, b)$: $\sum_i \lambda_1(|a_i| + |b_i|) + \lambda_2|a_i - b_i|$, the penalty of the fused graphical lasso for two groups. Its proximal operator first solves the fusion part, moving $a_i$ and $b_i$ toward each other by $\gamma\lambda_2$ or to their mean. It then soft thresholds both by $\gamma\lambda_1$. Composing the two proximal operators this way is exact (Friedman, Hastie, Höfling and Tibshirani, 2007).

Spectral penalties

For a function of the singular values, $F(X) = f(\sigma(X))$, with $f$ absolutely symmetric, the proximal operator acts on the singular values: $\operatorname{prox}_{\gamma F}(X) = U\operatorname{diag}(\operatorname{prox}_{\gamma f}(\sigma))V^\top$ for $X = U\operatorname{diag}(\sigma)V^\top$ (Lewis, 1995). For NuclearNorm, $f = \lambda\|\cdot\|_1$, which gives singular value thresholding. TracePSD, $\lambda\operatorname{tr}(X)$ on the positive semidefinite matrices, is the analogous function of the eigenvalues: it subtracts $\gamma\lambda$ from each eigenvalue and clips at zero. It is the penalty on the low-rank part of latent-variable graphical models. value_and_prox! gets the value from the thresholded spectrum. The decompositions run in LAPACK workspaces that the functions own.
