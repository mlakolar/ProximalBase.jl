Range clauses

The shared range guarantees describe the checks referenced here.

GroupNormL2 values apply the same check to their group terms λ0*w[k]*‖x[G_k]‖₂, and recompute a sum below K*floatmin(T) for K groups from the terms' exponents.

The proximal operators of GroupNormL2 recompute, for finite inputs, a group norm whose sum of squares overflows or underflows and a threshold γ*λ0*w[k] outside the range, in power-of-two units; the ordinary path needs a normal threshold and a normal sum of squares, and is otherwise unchanged.

The common interpretation of infinite penalties at zero step applies. A finite zero group becomes +0 under positive finite parameters.

Proximal result at an infinite step

For a group with a positive finite level and weight, the infinite step γ = Inf makes its proximal result +0 throughout.

Values at infinite weights or levels

An infinite-weight or infinite-level group's value is zero at a zero group, NaN when it contains NaN, and Inf otherwise; a zero weight overrides these value rules and contributes zero.

Finite-input range recovery

For finite inputs, norms whose sums of squares overflow or underflow and thresholds outside the floating-point range are recomputed in power-of-two units, so that a representable proximal point is returned accurately. Values recover terms that round below floatmin: for Float32 and Float64, a sum below K*floatmin, where K is the total number of groups, is recomputed using term exponents. Values at or above floatmax/2 are recomputed in power-of-two units and accumulated in at least Float64. Within a few eps of the overflow threshold the result may be floatmax or Inf, regardless of which one the exact value rounds to. See value range limits.

GroupNormL2 does not apply to a SymmetricSparseIterate (its proximal operators throw an ArgumentError before anything is written), and it has no restrict, no per-group step and no compact block method under Scaled. With a positive finite α, Scaled(GroupNormL2(...), α) multiplies the value by α, computes the array proximal operator with the step αγ, and scores a group by evaluating the score of GroupNormL2 on v/α and multiplying it by α (see the scaling guide for the limits and the unsupported cases); test/kkt_block.jl checks these scores against the definition of the scaled function. A zero α throws an ArgumentError, and an infinite α has no endpoint rule of its own. The group-lasso example checks the value, the proximal operators, the block operations and the screen against their formulas.
