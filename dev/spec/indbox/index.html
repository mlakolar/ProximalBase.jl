IndBox numerical contract

This page gathers the existing numerical rules for IndBox. The text is preserved from the earlier manual. Clause conversion and status assignments are a separate review step; unstated status remains unclassified. Read How to read the numerical contracts for authority and ownership.

Identity

Links only until clause conversion. IndBox owns the box projection rules. IndBallLinf is its thin constructor with bounds -r and r; its cases are retained in Endpoint clauses.

Source: src/functions/indbox.jl. The API coverage matrix lists the interface.

Endpoint clauses

CaseRetained behavior
IndBoxExact closed intervals, with no feasibility tolerance. A NaN entry gives value NaN and stays NaN under projection. An infinite entry is feasible only at an unbounded endpoint in its direction. The projection clamps each entry independently of the step and the fused value is evaluated at the stored result. Public scalar-step array oracles reject negative and NaN steps before writes, including after nonnegative scaling.
IndBallLinfInherits the IndBox contract with bounds -r and r. At zero radius, non-NaN entries project to zero with the sign inherited from clipping. At infinite radius both infinite endpoints are feasible. Negative, NaN and positive unsigned radii throw ArgumentError.
