and records further numerical defects and simplifications.

## Architecture evidence

- Inheritance and capability are distinct. `SqrNormL2` and wrappers directly subtype AbstractFunction yet can advertise separability; `SeparableFunction` is a reusable implementation family.
- Scalar kernels are `prox_entry`/`value_entry`; full array loops and coordinate operations dispatch separately on storage. A BoxConstrained scalar kernel calls inner scalar prox and then box clamp; C04 checks the original interval intersection and the working-precision clamped point at this exact boundary.
- Conjugate prox calls inner prox through Moreau decomposition and owns scratch. Its standalone value remains implemented only for listed inner types. The repaired value_and_prox! adds Fenchel equality at the proximal output for other proximable conjugates and retains existing closed-form values.
- Precompose owns image, inner and direction buffers; its adjoint is a numerical call, not an inheritance relationship. Regularize transforms point/step before calling inner prox.
- LinearConstrained owns mutable multiplier/slope caches, six scratch holders and optional compact live-entry state; warm and cold searches share the same root equation.
- AffineConstrained composes a loss and IndAffine, reuses loss decompositions and owns a step-dependent Schur factor; reset_cache! crosses both cache layers.
- GLMLoss Hessian products call ScalarLoss.deriv2, while quadratic_model! constructs a weighted Squared GLMLoss. Floors modify model curvature while preserving its gradient at the model point (subject to numerical precision).

## Deferred validation and residual risks
