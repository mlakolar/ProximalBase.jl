Endpoint clauses

CaseRetained behavior
IndNonnegativeValue 0 at a point with no negative and no NaN entry (-0.0 and +Inf included), Inf at a negative entry (-Inf included) when no entry is NaN, NaN at a NaN entry. The projection is max(v, 0) at a step γ ≥ 0: -0.0 and -Inf give +0, NaN stays NaN. A negative or NaN scalar step throws ArgumentError before any write in prox, prox! and value_and_prox!; another check (argument axes, shape or type) can raise its error first; array-step entries and prox_block do not check and project. The same scalar-step check applies after nonnegative scaling. value_and_prox! returns the value at the stored output: NaN at a NaN output entry, otherwise 0. The tolerance of the archived value (v >= -_tol) is not kept: a negative entry of any size gives Inf
